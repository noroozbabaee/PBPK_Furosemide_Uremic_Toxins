\caption{%
\textbf{Analytical sensitivity of PBUT total plasma accumulation to
furosemide $K_i$ and residual OAT1 $V_{\max}$}, under chronic oral
furosemide TID ($3\times80$\,mg/day) in CKD-5.
Colour is the closed-form percentage change $\Delta C_{ss}$ (\%) versus
the no-drug CKD-5 state.
\textbf{A)}~IS, \textbf{B)}~HA and \textbf{C)}~pCS share one colour
scale for $\Delta C_{ss}$.
The $x$-axis is the furosemide OAT1 inhibition constant $K_i$ (mg/L,
log scale).
The $y$-axis is basolateral OAT1 $V_{\max}$ as a \emph{fraction of the
healthy value} (the CKD-5 model scale $0.05$ is one point on this axis,
not the unit of the axis).
The black star is the model point ($K_i=0.35$\,mg/L, $V_{\max}$ scale
$=0.05$), using the ODE last-cycle mean plasma furosemide
$\overline{C}_{\mathrm{furo}}=5.31$\,mg/L (TID $3\times80$\,mg/day;
not $1.68$\,mg/L). The closed-form colour at the star uses that constant
$\overline{C}$; the 30-day ODE at the same regimen is IS $+28.1$\,\%.
No isolines are drawn.
Unbound furosemide is $C_u=\overline{C}_{\mathrm{furo}}\,f_{u,p}$ (no extra
$BP$ division).
This map is not the single-dose IV ODE heatmap.
}
\label{fig:ki_vmax_heatmap}
