\caption{%
\textbf{Closed-form sensitivity of PBUT plasma accumulation to unbound
fractions} under chronic oral furosemide TID $3\times80$\,mg/day in CKD-5.
Colour is analytical $\Delta C_{ss}$ (\%) relative to the no-drug CKD-5
baseline, \emph{not} a pixel-wise ODE (the 30-day PBPK predictions are
Figure~5).
Panels: IS (\textbf{A}), HA (\textbf{B}), pCS (\textbf{C}).
The map uses a single mean plasma furosemide
$\overline{C}_{\mathrm{furo}}=1.68$\,mg/L (ODE last-cycle average at this
regimen) with $C_u=\overline{C}_{\mathrm{furo}}\,f_{u,p}$ and
$K_i=0.35$\,mg/L; inhibition is Michaelis--Menten (Supplementary S6).
$x$: furosemide $f_{u,p}$ (0.01--0.15).
$y$: toxin $f_{u,p}$ obtained by scaling the Deltombe CKD-5 values
(IS $0.10$, HA $0.52$, pCS $0.05$) from $0.4\times$ to $2.5\times$.
Black star: model operating point ($f_{u,p}^{\mathrm{furo}}=0.05$ at the
Deltombe toxin $f_{u,p}$).
HA remains small because residual OAT1 is a minor fraction of HA $CL_R$.
}
