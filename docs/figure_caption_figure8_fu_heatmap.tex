\caption{%
\textbf{Two-dimensional sensitivity of PBUT total plasma accumulation to
furosemide and toxin plasma unbound fractions}, under chronic oral
furosemide TID ($3\times80$\,mg/day) in CKD-5.
Colour is the closed-form percentage change $\Delta C_{ss}$ (\%) in
time-averaged total plasma concentration relative to the no-drug CKD-5
state, for IS (\textbf{A}), HA (\textbf{B}) and pCS (\textbf{C}).
The $x$-axis is furosemide $f_{u,p}$ (0.01--0.15).
The $y$-axis is the toxin $f_{u,p}$ after scaling the Deltombe CKD-5
value (IS $0.10$, HA $0.52$, pCS $0.05$) over $0.4$--$2.5\times$.
The black star is the model point ($f_{u,p}^{\mathrm{furo}}=0.05$ at the
Deltombe toxin $f_{u,p}$).
No isolines are drawn.
Unbound furosemide in the map is $C_{u}=C_{\mathrm{ss,plasma}}\,f_{u,p}$
(plasma $C_{\mathrm{ss}}$; no extra $BP$ division).
$K_i=0.35$\,mg/L.
}
